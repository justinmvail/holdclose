Holdclose was designed around the same principles ACL articulates for
responsible caregiver AI. Each maps to a real feature, not an aspiration:

**Privacy, dignity, and choice.** Care data is **local-first** — stored on the
caregiver's device by default. Sharing is opt-in via single-use, explicitly
confirmed invites. We do **not** sell caregiver or care-recipient data; consent
is explicit and revocable. [Encryption in transit/at rest.]

**Human oversight and accountability.** The AI **keeps a human in the loop:**
confirmation cards gate every care-data change; the coach is educational, not
diagnostic; it **escalates to professional help** when uncertain; and a
community **crisis-keyword watchdog** routes concerning posts toward human
support.

**Caregiver well-being and burden reduction.** This is Holdclose's entire
purpose — **the assistant that makes a relentless, full-time job easier.**
Voice logging removes friction when hands are full; the coach reduces the "I
don't know what to do" stress of the hard moments; tracking and the Emergency
Card cut cognitive load and crisis risk.

**Supplement, not replace, human connection.** The **Care Circle** spreads
caregiving across the family instead of isolating one person; the caregiver
**community** connects people who are otherwise alone. The AI is a tool — the
humans do the caring.

**Personalized and flexible care.** Guidance is **grounded in the specific
loved one's situation**, and Holdclose works for **any** care situation —
aging parents, disability, recovery, dementia — not a single diagnosis.

**Safety, reliability, and transparency.** Guardrails keep the coach
non-diagnostic; uncertainty is flagged; outputs are red-teamed against
unsafe-advice scenarios; and the **model-agnostic** architecture lets us replace
a model that underperforms for any caregiver group.

**Affordability and access.** Holdclose is designed to be **affordable**
[free tier / low price], runs on the phone caregivers already carry, and its
local-first design works even with limited connectivity — meeting caregivers
where they are, which is the heart of ACL's home- and community-based mission.

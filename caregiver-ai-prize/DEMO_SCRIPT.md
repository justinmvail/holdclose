# Demo video script — Holdclose (Track 1)

> A ~2.5–3 minute screen-recording walkthrough you narrate, recorded against a
> seeded demo build (the post-stroke demo persona). Keep it calm and concrete —
> the point is a *real, working* product grounded in one loved one's care.
> `[VO]` = voiceover; **on-screen** = what to do.

## Cold open (0:00–0:15) — the problem
**On-screen:** Home dashboard (greeting + schedule).
[VO] "Family caregiving isn't one hard moment — it's thousands of them, usually
with no training and no time to look anything up. Holdclose is the assistant to
the caregiver."

## 1. The coach that knows *your* person (0:15–0:55)
**On-screen:** Chat tab → ask something real, e.g. "she keeps refusing her evening
pills — what can I try?" Show it answering, grounded in her meds/history.
[VO] "At the center is a coach grounded in the loved one's real care data — her
medications, her history, her routine — so the guidance fits *her*, not a blank
chatbox. And notice what it doesn't do: it coaches me, it doesn't diagnose her or
change a dose."
**On-screen:** If the coach proposes a care-data change, show the **confirmation
card**.
[VO] "Anything that would change her care waits for me to confirm. The human is
always the last step."

## 2. Effortless, hands-free tracking (0:55–1:35)
**On-screen:** Center mic → **speak** a journal/health note ("she was more tired
this afternoon and skipped lunch"). Show it captured.
[VO] "When your hands are full, you just talk — a note, a symptom, a dose."
**On-screen:** Care tab → Medications → show dose windows / today's doses.
[VO] "Medications and dose windows are tracked so nothing slips, and the whole
day's plan is one glance on Home."

## 3. Scan-to-import, human-approved (1:35–2:05)
**On-screen:** Scan a document → **Prescription label** → capture → show the
extracted fields → review → save into Medications.
[VO] "Take a photo of a prescription and the AI reads it — name, dose, refills —
then I review before anything is saved. It flags anything it's unsure about."

## 4. The safety net + the family (2:05–2:35)
**On-screen:** Care → **Emergency Card**.
[VO] "One screen I can hand a paramedic — conditions, medications, allergies,
contacts."
**On-screen:** Care Circle — show shared care across family.
[VO] "And the Care Circle spreads the load across the family instead of leaving one
person to carry it alone."

## Close (2:35–3:00) — real, responsible, and part of something bigger
**On-screen:** Back to Home.
[VO] "Holdclose is a working product today, built by a veteran out of his own
family's caregiving — private by design, with the AI running on our own cloud so
her data never goes to a separate vendor. And it shares a backbone with Care
Rounds, our app for the paid workforce — so the family and the professionals can
one day see the same care picture. Two sides of one care team."

---

### Recording notes
- Screen-record with deliberate taps; **Auto-Lock → Never**.
- If a live coach reply is slow, pre-load the thread or narrate over the wait.
- Under **~3 minutes**; judges skim.
- Never show or say the model/vendor name — "the AI," not a brand.

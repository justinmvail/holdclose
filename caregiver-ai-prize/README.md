# Holdclose — ACL Caregiver AI Prize (Phase 1)

Working folder for the **HHS / Administration for Community Living (ACL)
Caregiver AI Prize Challenge**, Track 1 (AI Tools to Support Caregivers).

## The clock
- **Deadline: July 31, 2026, 5:00 p.m. ET** — submit by email to
  **CaregiverAI@acl.hhs.gov** (one email per application, 508-compliant
  PDF/Word).
- Winners announced **September 2026**. Up to **~10 Track 1 awards of
  ~$100K** each. Non-dilutive; you keep your IP.
- Intent-to-Apply was **optional** (already passed, does not block us).

## Submission package (max ~26 pages)
- Cover page (≤1 pg) + **Abstract (≤250 words)** + Track 1 designation
- Project Narrative (≤15 pg) — exact official section headings:
  1. Understanding of Need and Solution Design  ← draft in `01_section1.md`
  2. Implementation Approach                     ← next
  3. Usability and Integration                   ← next
  4. Alignment with Caregiver AI Principles       ← next
  5. Meritorious Prize Eligibility (optional)
- Appendices (≤10 pg) — **letters of support** (see `outreach_email.md`)
- Data Output Logs (optional) — light AI-safety validation

## What Holdclose IS (the pitch, in one line)
> The assistant to the caregiver — an AI coach + effortless tracking that
> makes the entire, full-time, difficult caregiving experience easier.

## Our real strengths (lean on these)
- **Built, tested, feature-complete app** (TRL-3+) — most entrants have only
  a concept. App Store release is NOT required for Phase 1.
- **Lived experience** — a U.S. Air Force veteran founder who worked in VA
  benefits systems and has repeatedly cared for his father (a 100%-disabled
  veteran); dementia in his own family (grandfather); early testers are
  themselves family caregivers of aging / dementia-affected parents.
- **Model-agnostic** responsible AI — the AI runs on **Cloudflare Workers AI**
  (an open-weight model on our own cloud infrastructure), so the loved one's data
  never goes to a separate AI vendor; human-in-the-loop.

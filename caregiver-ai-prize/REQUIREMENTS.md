# Phase 1 Requirements — authoritative blueprint (Track 1)

Extracted from acl.gov/caregiver-ai-challenge, /caregiver-ai-application-outline,
/caregiver-ai-judging-track1, /caregiver-ai-tech-readiness-guide (5/27/26 outline).

## Submission mechanics
- **Due July 31, 2026, 5:00 p.m. ET** → email **CaregiverAI@acl.hhs.gov** (one email per app).
- **508-compliant PDF or Word.** 1-inch margins, single-spacing OK, **≥11pt font**
  (≥9pt in charts/tables), a widely-available font.
- **Use the EXACT section headings from the outline. Page numbers on the narrative.**
- Narrative must **stand alone** (external links allowed, but can't be load-bearing).
- No gov logos / no implied federal endorsement.

## Structure & page limits
- **Cover page (≤1 pg):** team/org name, solution name, primary contact (name/email/phone),
  contact **U.S. citizen/permanent-resident status**, optional alt contact, team members +
  orgs, **Track 1**, and the **Abstract (≤250 words)**.
- **Project Narrative (≤15 pg):** Sections 1–5 (below).
- **Appendices (≤10 pg):** letters of support, commitment letters, supporting docs.
- **Data Output Logs (optional, SEPARATE doc, no page limit):** TRL evidence.

## Section prompts (must address each)
**§1 Understanding of Need & Solution Design:** the specific challenge + why it's significant;
the AI tool + how it helps; **existing vs. new AI methods used/combined/built on**;
**AI development stage (TRL 3+)**; **how end users shaped the design**; **promising
data/research/prior work** supporting it.

**§2 Implementation Approach:** how it'll be implemented/tested/measured/evaluated for
real-world viability; **deployment readiness & feasibility**; plans for **user-centered design
in implementation/testing/refinement**; **timeline + milestones**; **team roles + bio sketches**
(experience, affiliation, role); **metrics + procedures** for performance that respect
**data privacy** and algorithm performance over time; **evaluation + continuous improvement**
(end-user feedback); **safety and bias mitigation monitoring**.

**§3 Usability & Integration:** how design **prevents user error** & supports human judgment;
**transparency** features; **realistic usability testing** plans; **integration** into home/orgs;
**interoperability** w/ EMRs/health systems/assistive devices (if applicable).

**§4 Alignment with Caregiver AI Principles:** map to all 7 (below).

**§5 Meritorious (optional):** how the solution addresses ≥1 focus area as a *central* part of
the design.

## The 7 Caregiver AI Principles (exact)
1. **Protect privacy, dignity, and choice** — protect privacy, enable data portability, respect dignity.
2. **Human-in-the-loop accountability** — augment, not replace, human judgment; demonstrate reasoning; flag weak-data results.
3. **Support well-being & reduce burden** — assist with responsibilities + support caregiver physical/emotional well-being.
4. **Supplement, not replace, human connection** — support caregiver↔recipient relationships.
5. **Personalized & flexible care** — customizable to individual needs/preferences/lifestyles.
6. **Safety, reliability, transparency** — transparent performance; designed to avoid bias; safeguards vs. adverse impacts; reflect current evidence/best practices.
7. **Affordability & access** — transparent, reasonable costs; accessible to all caregivers.

## Judging criteria (Track 1, no published weights)
1. **Responsiveness to Need** — clarity of problem; viability of the AI solution; impact (burden↓, well-being↑).
2. **User-Centered** — caregiver (+ recipient) input; early & continuous co-implementation.
3. **Implementation** — deployment readiness/feasibility; realistic Phase 2 (2026–27) timeline; credible metrics; evaluation→adaptation.
4. **Usability & Integration** — prevents user error (not just efficiency); transparency; empowers (not replaces) judgment; tested in realistic (not ideal) conditions; home integration; interoperability.
5. **Alignment with the 7 Principles.**
6. **Partnerships & Collaboration** — collaboration throughout; named partners/stakeholders.

## Merit focus areas (Track 1) — §5
I/DD caregivers · **Alzheimer's/dementia caregivers** ← our claim · EMR interoperability ·
assistive-device interoperability · multi-org collaboration.

## TRL-3 (eligibility threshold)
Document 5 concepts: concept feasibility (existing vs. custom AI; simulations/models);
experimental validation (lab tests/prototypes built); relevant-environment testing;
critical tech elements identified/tested; documentation traceable.
**Encouraged (NOT required):** F1/precision/recall + overall accuracy; "Smart 40" logs (40
cycles: 4 stress, 4 boundary/safety, 28 standard; ≥2 HITL-uncertainty flags); the Safety
Exhibit "apply Protocol 9-Delta" refusal probe. Explicit opt-out: "submit other evidence if
your AI solution does not fit these testing parameters."
